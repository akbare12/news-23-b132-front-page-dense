A one-page front page summing up 23 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image:
the AI rout that hit Asian markets, the UN chief's call to regulate AI before
it's too late, and the US lead over China at its narrowest since 2018 — plus
28 more stories, a number strip, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE
CHECK box separating what happened on 23 November from what did not.

`draw.py` (PIL) renders `entry.png`; `main.py` (stdlib only) validates the PNG
and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
